Building a Stronger Community Together

RIHULA MUKHOBOLA ASSOCIATION is a community development association based in Busia County. Our goal is to bring people together, encourage cooperation, support one another and create sustainable community development.

Through teamwork, transparency and accountability, we empower members to improve their lives while making a positive impact in our community.

Mission

To empower members through unity, leadership and community development.

Vision

To become a leading community association that transforms lives through collaboration.
